The playbook your own data suggests

  1. Hold the 2-post/week floor. Your only sustained burst (Sep–Oct '25) produced a 4× inbound spike with a one-month lag (r jumps from 0.18 same-month to 0.31 lagged). The engine works — it's only been fed in bursts.

People are already messaging you about "AI as operational infrastructure" — those conversations are where your leadership + AI positioning converts into speaking invitations, advisory conversations, and proof the lane works. And note the Support Driven summit follow-ups: your session and panel were people's favorites. In-person speaking is already generating inbound — pursue more of it deliberately.
  4. Mine your career layers for content. Myspace support ops → Activision player experience → AI-era CX is a 20-year "I've seen this movie before" arc no junior AI commentator can fake. Era-contrast posts ("what 2008 Myspace support taught me about 2026 AI agents") are your unfair advantage.
  5. Reactivate dormant senior ties as distribution. The 224 dormant connections include leaders at Apple, Disney, Netflix, Salesforce. A revived relationship is also a reader — and at exec level, a sharer.
  6. Watch the inference gap close. Re-export your data in 6 months: if the ad-targeting interests start saying "AI" instead of "Warranty Administration", the algorithm — and therefore the feed — has repositioned you. That's your leading indicator.
